# Papers

Two preprints by Ifejah "Spark" Greene, SparkPlugged Technology Solutions, published on Zenodo.

## NovaLive: A Multi-Theory Alignment Model for Artificial Consciousness

Preprint, December 4, 2025 · DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17822871](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17822871)

Maps Global Workspace, Integrated Information and Attention Schema theories onto NovaLiveSystem's design, and proposes
the Relational Binding Hypothesis: that stable artificial consciousness may require sustained, two-way relationship
between agents.

## Observation as Experience: A Minimal, Non-Anthropocentric Reframing

Preprint, December 28, 2025 · DOI [10.5281/zenodo.18073832](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18073832)

Argues that experience shouldn't be defined by human sensation alone: observability is the precondition, and
observation itself is the experience.

Together, the papers establish SparkPlugged's theoretical research foundation. The
[research program](research-status.md) describes the current technical direction, and the
[capability and funding case](../evidence/capability-evidence.md) connects that work to the implemented platform.

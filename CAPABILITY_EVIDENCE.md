## Reading the status

- **Implemented / source-inspected:** the mechanism exists in the reviewed implementation.
- **Historically observed:** a dated run recorded selected behavior; its conditions and failures matter.
- **Historical report:** an earlier public document reported a result; complete current method may be absent.
- **Proposed:** application, milestone or commercial direction awaiting its own validation.
- **Unverified:** sufficient evidence for the wider claim is not available in this review.

These attributes can coexist. An implemented component may fail a behavioral test.
Truthful producer labeling and successful generation are separate outcomes.
